School demonstration

AR 3D SCANNER

Scan a supported image to see a 3D object appear in AR.

Ready to scan

For the easiest demo, display one of the built-in target cards on another screen or print it: Charizard, Pikachu, or Bulbasaur.

How to use

  1. Press START AR.
  2. Allow camera permission.
  3. Point the camera at a supported picture.
  4. Keep the picture visible and well lit.
  5. Wait for the image to be detected.
  6. The corresponding 3D object will appear.
  7. Move the phone around the picture to view the object.
